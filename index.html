About MPC

The architectural coatings company of Makkah Paints.

Mega Pro Coatings Company (MPC) is a subsidiary of Makkah Paints, specialising in architectural coatings, decorative wall finishes and paint solutions for residential and commercial buildings.

Our expertise

Finish families across every facade

Stone-effect finishes
