Our manufacturing capabilities

Built with technology. Driven by expertise.

Horizontal mixers

Large horizontal mixing troughs with side-mounted drives and bottom discharge valves.

Agitated vessels

Stainless-steel vessels with top-mounted motors and gear drives.

Raised working platforms

Steel mezzanines with railings give access to vessels and controls.

Transfer & handling

Pumps, hoses and mobile stainless-steel tanks move material between stages.

Equipment controls

Control panels mounted beside the mixers and on the vessel platform.
