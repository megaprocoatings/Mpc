Choosing a wall finish for villas and residential buildings

Five practical questions to answer before you pick a texture and colour for an exterior wall.

Picking an exterior finish is easier when you decide a few things in order. These questions are the ones our sample walls are designed to help with.

1. What look do you want?

Natural stone, brick, a relief texture or a clean colour? Each of MPC’s four finish families answers one of these.

2. How large is the surface?

Large facades show texture and colour differently from a single feature wall. Look at samples at full scale where you can.

3. What surrounds it?

Consider window frames, railings, stone details and neighbouring buildings when choosing a tone.

4. Which surfaces are involved?

List the walls: facades, balconies, compound and boundary walls. This helps when asking for quantities and documents.

5. Which documents do you need?

Contractors and consultants usually need technical and safety data sheets. These are available on request.
