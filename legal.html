Privacy policy

This website does not collect or store personal information itself. When you use the contact form, the details you type are placed into a new message in your own email app; nothing is sent until you choose to send that email, and nothing is saved by this website.

The newsletter form checks your email address in your browser only; the address is not stored or sent anywhere.

To display the website, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and, on the Contact page, a map from Google Maps. Those services receive standard technical information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies. This website sets no cookies of its own.

For any question about how MPC handles information you send us by email or phone, contact us.
