A guide to MPC’s four finish families

Stone-effect, scratch textures, brick-effect and fine-texture colour finishes: what each looks like and where it fits.

MPC’s architectural range is organised into four finish families. Each one is shown on our sample walls so you can judge the real texture and colour, not a render.

Stone-effect finishes

A speckled, granite-look surface in light grey, dark grey and pink-toned stone. See stone-effect finishes.

Scratch & grooved textures

Vertical scratch and bark-style relief in terracotta, brown, beige and cream. See scratch textures.

Brick-effect finishes

A brick pattern with light joints and varied tones, in charcoal and red. See brick-effect finishes.

Fine-texture colour finishes

An even, fine-grained surface across a broad palette, from blush and sage to ochre and deep red. See fine-texture finishes.

Product names, technical data and colour codes for each family are available from our team. For anything you need, send us an inquiry.
