About MPC

Mega Pro Coatings Company is a Saudi-based subsidiary of Makkah Paints, focused on architectural coatings and decorative wall finishes for residential and commercial buildings.

Our story

Built on a Saudi manufacturing heritage.

Mega Pro Coatings (MPC) carries the architectural work of Makkah Paints forward as a dedicated company. Our finishes are made for residential buildings, villas, commercial buildings and architectural surfaces — from stone-effect and brick-effect coatings to scratch textures and fine-texture colour finishes.

MPC operates its own production equipment and draws on the background of its parent company, a Saudi manufacturer of advanced paints and coating systems since 1997 and an affiliate of AKA Group.
